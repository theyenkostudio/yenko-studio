## Publishing notes

- Link every article to the studio's relevant website, brand or software services page.
- Use practical local examples, such as a logistics company in Lagos or an accountant in Accra, instead of generic global examples.
- Add links between posts as they go live: the first two should link to each other, then each later article should link back to both foundational pieces.
- Do not frame AI discovery as a replacement for SEO. The durable work is still clear, useful information that people and search systems can verify.
